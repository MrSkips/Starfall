## Name
**Meteor Forge Tycoon** (recommended)
Backups: Star Forge Tycoon · Meteor Crash Tycoon · Comet Forge Tycoon
Why not "Starfall Forge": D-013. The style-reference video advertises a "Starfall Event", so the name is too close. "Tycoon" in the title helps search.

## Description (paste as-is)
Meteors are falling, and every one of them is worth Stardust!

Grab meteors as they crash into the crater, carry them back to your plot and drop them in your Crusher. Your production line turns rocks into ingots and glowing Star Bars, and you can watch every one ride the belts into your Stardust Silo.

BUILD your forge: Smelter, Forge, Star Anvil, Meteor Chute, Magnet Tower, Forge Drone and the giant Starforge Core.
RACE for rare meteors: Molten, Frozen, Charged and Cosmic are worth up to 12x. Meteor showers drop Starhearts that need two players (or a strong pet) to lift.
HATCH pets that help you play: they fetch meteors, call new ones down, upgrade your deliveries and keep your streak alive.
TEAM UP in Core Breach: every 18 minutes the giant Meteorite cracks open. Deal damage with your meteors and pets to earn Core Shards and event-only pets.
GO SUPERNOVA: reset for permanent payout boosts and extra pet slots.

Like and favorite so you hear about new updates!

## Settings
- Genre: Simulation → Subgenre: Tycoon
- Max players: 8 (one plot each)
- Devices: Computer, Phone, Tablet (add Console only after a controller test)
- Maturity questionnaire: no violence against characters, no blood, no gambling (no paid random items), free-text chat on. Expect the lowest rating ("Minimal").
- Private servers: on, free or 100 Robux (adds a little revenue)
